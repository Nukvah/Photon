# Photon privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Photon makes photos smaller so they can be sent by email. Everything happens on your phone.

## What Photon collects

Nothing. Photon has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. It does not
have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## Your photos

- Photon reads only the photos you share with it or pick in the photo picker.
- It writes the smaller copies to its own private storage on your phone and keeps only the
  latest batch: the copies are deleted when the next batch finishes, or sooner if Android
  clears the app's cache.
- Copies leave Photon only when you send them: to the email app you choose, to another app
  through Android's share sheet, or to a folder you pick. What happens next is up to that app.
- The smaller copies carry no metadata: no location, no camera details, no date taken.

## Settings Photon remembers

Photon stores on your phone the size you picked last, the email app you chose, and a short
summary of your last batch (file names, sizes and how long it took). It is never backed up
or sent anywhere, and uninstalling Photon removes it.

## Children

Photon collects no data from anyone, including children.
